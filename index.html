Our Story

A place that feels truly yours.

Happy Outdoor Solutions was founded by Roger Brul in 2018 with a simple idea — that everyone deserves a place outdoors that belongs to them. From compact A-Frame cabins to fully equipped Tiny House Containers, we build retreats that are practical, durable, and genuinely loved.

Whether it's a weekend getaway, an off-grid escape, or a backyard sanctuary, we help people create spaces where life slows down and happiness finds room to grow.

About Us

Built on a love of the outdoors — since 2018.

Happy Outdoor Solutions is a Maryland-based outdoor-living brand operating under Happy Watt LLC. Founded by Roger Brul in 2018, the company grew from a simple belief: that everyone deserves a space outdoors that feels like theirs.

From our workshop in Ridgely, we design and build Tiny House Containers, A-Frame cabins, garden planters and thoughtful outdoor products — each made to bring people closer to nature, comfort and creativity.

Our mission is simple. We help people create their own Happy Place.
